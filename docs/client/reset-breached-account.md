# Reset a Breached Account

If your firm offers it for your program, you can buy a **reset** after an account breaches and keep trading on a fresh account.

## What you get

- A **new account** on the same program, at the program's full starting balance.
- New login details, sent by email.
- The breached account stays as it is. Its history and the breach remain on record.
- Nothing is carried over from the old account: not the balance, not the profit.

## When the offer appears

The offer shows on the page of a **breached** account whose program has resets switched on. You will see "Reset your account for" followed by the price and a **Reset** button. The price is in the program's own currency.

You will not see the offer when:

- The account is not breached (for example it passed, or it is frozen).
- Your firm has not enabled resets for that program.
- That breached account has already been reset. One reset per breach.
- You used all the resets your firm allows on the program. When there is a limit, the offer shows how many you have left.

## Paying

1. Choose **Reset** on the breached account and pay.
2. If you close the payment window without paying, the offer returns after a short while (about 30 minutes) or as soon as the payment fails or expires.
3. When the payment is confirmed your new account is created, usually within a few minutes. Until then the page says your payment was received and the new account is being set up.

If your payment went through but no account appears after a while, contact support. Staff can see paid resets that have not produced an account and retry them.
