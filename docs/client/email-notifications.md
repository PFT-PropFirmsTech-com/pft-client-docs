# Email Notifications

The emails the platform sends you. Your firm can edit the wording and the language, so subjects may differ. Check your spam folder if one is missing.

## Account

- **Welcome**, after you register.
- **Account created for you**, when you buy at checkout with a new email, or when staff create your account. It contains a temporary password.
- **Verification code**, at sign-up and at every login, if your firm uses codes. A code is valid for 10 minutes. You cannot switch this on or off yourself.
- **Password reset**. The link is valid for 1 hour.

## Buying

- **Payment success**.
- **New trading account**, with your login details.
- **Pay After Pass**: a notice, reminders 7, 3 and 1 day before the window ends, and an expiry email.

## Progress

- **Stage passed** (phase 1, 2 or 3), **challenge passed or not passed**, **funded account achieved**.
- **Certificates** for a stage, funded status or a payout, if your firm uses them.
- **Program progression** when your next account is ready.

## Rules and breaches

- **Warnings** for minimum trade duration, stop loss, take profit, lot size and weekend holding, depending on your program.
- **Inactivity warning**, then a breach if you stay inactive.
- **Account breached** and, if offered, a **Rebound offer** or a **replacement account** email.

## Verification

- **KYC requested**, **validated**, **rejected**, and a reminder.
- **Contract requested**, **approved**, **rejected**.

## Payouts

Request received, approved and confirmed (sent together), rejected, or cancelled. See [Payout Rejected vs Cancelled](/client/reward-rejected-vs-cancelled).

## Other

- **Free trial** or **free challenge** ready.
- **Support ticket resolved**.
- **Affiliate**: a referral registered, a commission earned.
- **Abandoned checkout reminders**, if your firm sends them.

## Staying safe

Official emails come from your firm. Never share your password or verification code, and do not open links you did not expect.
