# Account Purchase

How to buy a program. Sizes, prices, add-ons and payment methods are your firm's choice, so the checkout page always shows the real values.

## Steps

1. **Choose a program and size.** Programs are explained in [Programs](/client/programs). The checkout shows the rules that apply, including the breach limits in the account currency.
2. **Pick add-ons** your firm sells, such as a higher profit split, different drawdown, faster payouts or a reset.
3. **Add a coupon** if you have one. Your firm may allow a second one.
4. **Review Package, Payment Details, Confirm Payment.** Enter your billing details and choose a payment method.

You can buy without registering first: checkout creates your account and emails a temporary password.

## Payment methods

Checkout offers the methods your firm has switched on, typically card, PayPal, crypto, or a local method such as UPI in India. Bank transfer is not a checkout method: if your firm accepts one, it records the payment for you.

## Pay After Pass

Some programs let you pay a smaller price now. The remaining price is due **once**, after you pass and before your funded account. The payment window is 14 days from the pass unless your firm changes it. Trading stays off until you pay. See [Account Outcomes](/client/account-outcomes).

## After you pay

- You get a payment confirmation email and, shortly after, an email with your trading account login details.
- Your payments appear on the Payment History page. A payment can be pending, expired or partially paid.
