# User Journey

The path from sign-up to payouts. Your firm sets the numbers and which steps apply.

1. **Sign up or buy.** Register, or buy at checkout, which creates your account. You may need an emailed code to verify your email and to log in, if your firm requires one. Google sign-in may be available. See [Account Purchase](/client/account-purchase).
2. **Trade a challenge stage.** Reach the profit target and other objectives without breaking a rule. See [Trading Experience](/client/trading-experience).
3. **Pass.** The stage is marked passed, and you receive a certificate email if your firm uses certificates. If there is a next stage you get a **new account** with new login details.
4. **Get verified.** Before your funded account is created, your KYC and your contract must be approved. Your firm may also review the pass manually.
5. **Trade funded.** Your profit split and payout rules come from your program.
6. **Request a payout** when you are eligible. See [Withdrawal Process](/client/withdrawal-process).

## If an account breaches

Trading stops and the account shows the reason. You can reset it (see [Reset a Breached Account](/client/reset-breached-account)), take a Rebound offer if you have one, or buy again. See [Account Outcomes](/client/account-outcomes).

## Other ways in

- A **free trial** is a demo account without payouts: [Free Trial Accounts](/client/free-trial).
- A **free challenge** or a **Pay After Pass** program changes the steps above slightly: your account page shows what applies.
