# Programs

A **program** is what you buy: an account size with its rules, stages and prices. Every firm builds its own programs, so sizes, prices, targets and limits differ between firms. This page explains how programs are put together. The checkout page and your account page show the exact values for yours.

## Challenge types

- **1-step, 2-step and 3-step**: one, two or three evaluation stages before a funded account.
- **Instant funding**: no evaluation, you start on a funded account.
- **Special** programs, built by your firm.

Stages run in order: phase 1, phase 2, phase 3, then funded. Each stage is its own account and you get new login details when you move on. Only the entry program is sold; later stages are issued when you pass.

## What a program sets

- Account size and starting balance
- Profit target for each stage and minimum trading days
- Daily and maximum loss limits, and the drawdown model (standard, trailing or hybrid)
- Other rules such as minimum trade duration, mandatory stop loss, lot size limits or weekend holding
- Profit split and payout rules for funded accounts
- Price, and whether it is a Pay After Pass program

## Other kinds of program

- **Pay After Pass**: a smaller price now, the rest before your funded account.
- **Free trial**: a demo account that does not pay out. See [Free Trial Accounts](/client/free-trial).
- **Free challenge**: a challenge you do not pay for, with its own limits.
- **Short-term challenge**: a fixed-length challenge that ends in a reward claim.

## Add-ons

Add-ons change a program when you buy it, for example a higher profit split, different drawdown, faster payouts or a reset. Which add-ons exist is your firm's choice.

## Where to see the rules

At checkout, the panel "Trading Rules & Immediate Breach Conditions" lists the rules that apply to the program you are buying, with limits in the account currency. The same rules are on your account page.
