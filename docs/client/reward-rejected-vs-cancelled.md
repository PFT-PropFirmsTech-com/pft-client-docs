# Payout Rejected vs Cancelled

A payout request can end in two ways other than being paid: **rejected** or **cancelled**. They are not the same. This page explains the difference and what to do next.

## At a glance

- **Rejected**: your firm reviewed the request and did not accept it, usually because of a rule or compliance problem. A balance deduction may apply.
- **Cancelled**: the request was withdrawn without a verdict on your trading. It is neutral: no penalty, and you can submit a new request straight away.

Trading on the account is switched back on in both cases.

## Rejected

A rejection means the review found a problem such as a broken trading rule, suspicious activity, or a verification or contract requirement that was not met.

**What it can cost you.** By default a rejection applies a balance deduction to the account. Staff can instead choose to reset the account without a penalty, for example when the rejection was an administrative error. Read your rejection email and check your account balance afterwards to see which applied.

**What you can do**

1. Read the reason in the rejection email.
2. Contact support if you think it was a mistake.
3. Fix whatever was missing (verification, contract, rule issue).
4. Request again when you are eligible.

## Cancelled

A cancellation is neutral. Common causes:

- **You cancelled it** from the dashboard within 24 hours of submitting. See [Withdrawal Process](/client/withdrawal-process#cancel-a-request).
- **Staff cancelled it** because payout details were wrong (wrong wallet address, wrong bank or Revolut details).
- **Administrative correction**: staff voided the request so it could be redone.

Your balance is not affected. Correct the details if that was the cause and submit a new request.

## The emails

Default email subjects are shown below. Your firm may edit the wording.

- Request received: "Payout Request Received"
- Approved: "Payout Approved", then "Payout Confirmed"
- Rejected: "Payout Request Rejected"
- Cancelled: "Your Withdrawal Request Has Been Cancelled"

Approval and confirmation arrive together, at the moment the request is approved.
