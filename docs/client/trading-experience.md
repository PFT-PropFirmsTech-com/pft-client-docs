# Trading Experience

You trade on MT5 (or MatchTrader, if your firm offers it). The dashboard is where you watch your account, your objectives and your rules. It does not place or change trades.

## Your account page

Open **Accounts** and choose an account. The page shows, live while you have it open:

- Balance, equity, open positions and trade history.
- Statistics such as win rate, best trade and averages, and a daily profit and loss calendar.
- **Objective cards**, each marked Passed, Failed, Ongoing or Upcoming: profit target, trading days, daily loss, maximum loss and any other rules your program uses.
- A **margin** card with colour bands at 50% and 80% of margin used.
- Cards for trade size consistency and similar rules, when your program has them.

On each closed trade you can save a profit and loss card as an image. Exporting the full history is not available to traders.

## Rules and warnings

Your program decides which rules apply. The rules and breach conditions are shown at checkout and on your account. Many rules send a **warning email** before they can become a breach. Check your email and read your rules before you trade.

## While a payout is pending

When you submit a payout request, trading on that account is switched off until the request is decided. See [Withdrawal Process](/client/withdrawal-process).

## Tools

The Trading menu has more tools, depending on what your firm enables:

- Trading Journal
- Economic Calendar
- Lot Size Calculator
- Leaderboard

## Notifications

In-app notices cover payments, KYC, contracts, payouts and support. Rule warnings and results arrive by email: see [Email Notifications](/client/email-notifications).
