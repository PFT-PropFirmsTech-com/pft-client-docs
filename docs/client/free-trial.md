# Free Trial Accounts

Some firms offer a free trial: a demo account to try the platform before buying a challenge.

## What to expect

- You start from the free trial page. The form verifies your email address first.
- The trial is a **demo account**. It is for practice and does not pay out.
- The Withdrawals page shows "Payouts are not available for free trial accounts" and has no request button.
- To get paid you need a funded account, which you reach by buying and passing a challenge.

## Free trial vs free challenge

A **free challenge** is a different product. It is a challenge you do not pay for, with its own rules and limits set by your firm. Check the program page for what applies to it, including any payout limit.
