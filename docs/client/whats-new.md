# What's New and Navigation

## Changelog

The **Changelog** page lists recent improvements to the dashboard. You do not need to sign in to read it.

- Releases use **date-based versions**, for example `2026.11.13`: the day the change was published.
- Each release groups its items by area, such as Trader, Admin, Checkout, Withdrawals or Navigation.
- The latest version also appears at the bottom of the sidebar. Select it to open the Changelog.
- Items are written for users. Internal changes are left out.

## Collapsible sidebar

The sidebar can shrink to a narrow icon rail to give the page more room.

- Use the toggle at the top of the sidebar to collapse or expand it. Your choice is remembered **in that browser**.
- In the rail, hover an icon to see its name. Menu groups with sub-pages open a small panel when you select them.
- On narrower screens (below 1024 px wide) the rail stays in place and the full sidebar opens on top of the page. It closes when you press **Escape**, select outside it, or open a page.
