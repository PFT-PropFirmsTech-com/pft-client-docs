# New Firm Onboarding Checklist

What a firm decides and supplies before launch. This page lists the decisions; it never needs a secret in writing.

> **Send credentials only through the secure channel agreed with your account manager.** Do not put MT5 manager passwords, payment keys or API tokens in a shared document, chat or spreadsheet.

## Brand and legal

- Firm name, logo, colours and favicon
- Dashboard and website addresses
- Terms, privacy and legal page links
- Support channels and community links

## Trading platform

- MT5 server, manager access and the MT5 groups your programs use
- Trading timezone (day boundaries use it) and server time offset
- Whether you offer MatchTrader accounts

## Programs

- Challenge types, account sizes, prices and stages
- Rules per program: drawdown model and limits, profit targets, minimum days, other rules and whether each warns or breaches
- Profit split and payout rules per payout number
- Add-ons, bundles and coupons
- Pay After Pass, free trial, free challenge, paid reset and Rebound, if you use them

## Payments and payouts

- Payment gateways you will switch on, and the credentials to enter in payment configuration
- Payout methods you will offer: crypto, bank transfer, Revolut Tag, Rise Pay
- Payout caps and the review process

## Accounts and verification

- Whether traders need an emailed code at sign-up and login
- KYC provider and what counts as approved
- Contract: your own upload template or a system contract with signature fields
- Funded account approval: automatic or manual

## Email and notifications

- SMTP provider and sender address
- Review of the email templates and their languages
- Certificate templates, if you issue certificates
- Trustpilot and any review funnel

## Launch check

Make a test purchase, pass a stage on a test program, and run one payout request through approval before opening to traders.
