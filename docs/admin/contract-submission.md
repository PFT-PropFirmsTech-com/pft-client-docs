# Contract Submission

How contracts work for traders and how staff review them.

## One contract per trader

A contract belongs to the **trader**, not to a program. One approved contract unlocks every account of that trader.

## When it is needed

- Before a **funded account** is created (together with approved KYC).
- Before a **payout request** is accepted.
- On Pay After Pass programs, after the remaining price is paid.

## Two kinds

- **Uploaded contract**: the trader uploads a signed file. PDF, Word and image files up to 10 MB are accepted; there is no page-count or minimum-size check.
- **System contract**: you upload a template with signature fields and the trader signs it in the dashboard. It can be assigned to traders automatically at registration. An external e-signature integration is not active.

## Statuses

A contract is **pending**, **approved** or **rejected**. A rejection needs a reason, which is sent to the trader. A trader who already has an approved contract cannot upload another.

> Rejecting a system contract deletes the signed copies of the users it was assigned to.

## Approval

- Staff review contracts in the admin menu: **User Contracts** and **System Contracts**.
- A firm-wide **auto-approve** setting (off by default) approves every upload as soon as it arrives. It is all-or-nothing: there are no conditions.
- Staff can request a contract from one trader or in bulk; the trader gets a contract request email.

## Emails

Contract requested, approved and rejected. Edit the wording in your email templates.
