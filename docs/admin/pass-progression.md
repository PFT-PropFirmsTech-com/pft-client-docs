# Pass & Progression

What happens when an account reaches its objectives, how it moves to the next stage, and where staff can hold or approve it. Targets, days, limits, stage count and prices are all set per program.

## When an account passes

An account passes when it reaches the profit target and its other objectives, and it has not been breached: the account must not be banned and must never have exceeded its drawdown limits. Warnings do not block a pass.

On a pass the system:

1. Marks the account passed, disables trading and closes open positions.
2. Sends the stage-passed email, and a certificate email if you have an active certificate template of that type.
3. Creates the next stage as a **new account** with new login details, if there is one.

Certificates are images rendered from the template you upload (name, date, amount, account type, optional QR code) with a verification hash. The feature can be off for your firm.

## Auto or manual progression

One firm-wide setting decides whether passed accounts move on by themselves (auto) or wait for staff (manual). In manual mode the account shows as passed and **in review**; staff progress it from **Review Progression** in the admin menu, or breach it. There is no reject, appeal or second-review step, and no admin notification is sent. Free trial and free challenge stages skip the review.

## Before a funded account is created

- The trader's **KYC** must be approved (a request email goes out if it is not).
- The trader's **contract** must be signed and approved.
- You can also hold funded accounts for manual approval in the **Funded Queue**.

The account stays passed until those are done.

## Pay After Pass

On Pay After Pass programs the trader pays an initial price at purchase and the **remaining price once**, before the funded account. Both prices are fixed per program and the initial price can be zero.

- The payment window starts at the pass: 14 days by default. Staff can extend it for a trader.
- Reminders go out 7, 3 and 1 day before the window ends, and an expiry email after.
- After expiry a discounted reactivation offer is possible.
- Your firm can switch on card-on-file auto-charge for the remaining fee.

## Other progression paths

- **Instant** programs have no challenge stage.
- **Short-term challenges** end in a reward claim instead of a next stage.
- Add-ons bought for a stage can carry over to the next stage.
