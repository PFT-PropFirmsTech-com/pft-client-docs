<objective>
Close the external DOS report (umer@barhamsec.com, target app.xpips.com/auth/register): unbounded password input lets an attacker submit multi-MB passwords, amplifying CPU-bound bcrypt hashing per request and enabling a DOS via concurrent registrations.

Enforce a server-side `.max(72)` byte-safe ceiling on every user-supplied password field so oversized input is rejected with a 422 by the `validateRequest` Zod middleware BEFORE the controller reaches `bcrypt.hash`/`bcrypt.compare`. 72 is the OWASP bcrypt-safe ceiling (bcrypt silently truncates at 72 bytes anyway), so this also removes a latent silent-truncation correctness bug.

Purpose: Global security fix across all white-label brands (shared backend code, no per-brand config).
Output: One-file change to auth.validation.ts adding `.max(72, ...)` to 4 fields.
</objective>

Message string to use everywhere: `"Password must be at most 72 characters long"`.

1. registerUserValidationSchema.password (currently line 12):
   FROM: `password: z.string().min(6, "Password must be at least 6 characters long").nonempty("Password is required"),`
   TO:   `password: z.string().min(6, "Password must be at least 6 characters long").max(72, "Password must be at most 72 characters long").nonempty("Password is required"),`

2. loginUserValidationSchema.password (currently line 52):
   FROM: `password: z.string().min(6, "Password must be at least 6 characters long"),`
   TO:   `password: z.string().min(6, "Password must be at least 6 characters long").max(72, "Password must be at most 72 characters long"),`

3. changePasswordValidationSchema.currentPassword (currently line 64):
   FROM: `currentPassword: z.string().min(6, "Password must be at least 6 characters long"),`
   TO:   `currentPassword: z.string().min(6, "Password must be at least 6 characters long").max(72, "Password must be at most 72 characters long"),`

4. changePasswordValidationSchema.newPassword (currently line 65):
   FROM: `newPassword: z.string().optional(),`
   TO:   `newPassword: z.string().max(72, "Password must be at most 72 characters long").optional(),`
   (newPassword stays .optional() — do NOT add a .min() here; adding a min is a separate pre-existing weakness flagged in RESEARCH.md and is OUT OF SCOPE. .max on an optional string only validates when the field is present, which is correct — an absent newPassword must still pass.)

5. forgotPasswordValidationSchema.newPassword (currently line 103):
   FROM: `newPassword: z.string().min(8, "Password must be at least 8 characters long").nonempty("New password is required"),`
   TO:   `newPassword: z.string().min(8, "Password must be at least 8 characters long").max(72, "Password must be at most 72 characters long").nonempty("New password is required"),`

Chain order note: put `.max(72, ...)` AFTER the existing `.min(...)` and BEFORE any `.nonempty(...)` / `.optional()`, exactly as shown above. Zod evaluates all string checks regardless of order, but keep the shown order for readability.

That is 5 field-edits across 4 password fields (changePassword has two: currentPassword + newPassword). Do not modify OTP, email, token, refreshToken, or any non-password field.
