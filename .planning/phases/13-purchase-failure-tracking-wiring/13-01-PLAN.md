<objective>
Build the reusable foundation every failure/expiry call site depends on, in three tightly-coupled edits:
1. Extend the existing `TrackingEvents.purchaseFailed()` helper so it accepts an `eventId` (for retry-safe dedup) and a `reason` (richer than the bare `errorCode`), mirroring how `purchaseCompleted` already accepts `eventId`.
2. Fix the Klaviyo adapter's `buildProperties()` to promote `reason` and `errorCode` into the outgoing Klaviyo `properties` — they are currently silently dropped even though the fields exist on the payload type.
3. Create a new shared `emitTrackingPurchaseFailed(payment, errorCode?)` util that mirrors the proven `emitTrackingPurchaseCompleted` pattern (loose PaymentLike, normalized USD amount, stable `eventId`, fire-and-forget try/catch).

Purpose: Without this foundation, every downstream call site would hand-roll the emit inline (field-mapping drift, missing eventId, JPY-as-USD risk) and the reason data would never reach Klaviyo. Centralizing it in one util + fixing the adapter once is the correct, low-drift shape — exactly how v1.3 shipped `emitTrackingPurchaseCompleted`.
Output: One new util file; two surgical edits to the helper and the Klaviyo adapter.
</objective>
